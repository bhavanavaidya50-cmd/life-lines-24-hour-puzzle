# GAMEPLAY & STRATEGY GUIDE
## LIFE LINES – THE 24-HOUR PUZZLE

---

### 1. General Tactical Strategy

1. **Investigate Before Committing**: Never jump straight into scheduling tasks without reading the discovered evidence in the Investigation Scene. Documents often reveal early closing hours (e.g. Maple Pharmacy closes at 13:00, First National Bank closes at 15:00) and prerequisites.
2. **Watch Prerequisite Chains**: Tasks with dependencies cannot be completed out of sequence. For instance, in Casefile 01, Dr. Vance's telehealth consultation must occur before picking up antibiotics at Maple Pharmacy.
3. **Use the Auto-Optimize Tool for Reference**: If you find yourself stuck with complex schedule collisions, click `AUTO-OPTIMIZE` on the Planning Board. The game's scheduling algorithm will lay down a mathematically valid itinerary that you can inspect or fine-tune.
4. **Preserve Relationships & Keep Stress Below 65%**: Over-focusing on work or unilateral decisions spikes stress and damages relationships. High stress heavily penalizes your final score on the Consequence screen.

### 2. Walkthrough: Casefile 01 – The Unexpected Monday

- **Goal**: Balance Alex's regulatory corporate audit presentation with emergency medical care for their child and hospitalized father.
- **Recommended Investigation**: Read the *St. Jude Voicemail* to understand your father's condition, the *Thermometer Log* for your child's medication requirements, and *Marcus's Slack Chat*.
- **Recommended Timeline**:
  - `08:00 - 09:00`: Pediatric Doctor Telehealth Visit
  - `09:00 - 11:00`: Compile Regulatory Audit Deck
  - `11:00 - 12:00`: Brief Marcus & Handover Slides
  - `12:00 - 13:00`: Emergency Pharmacy Pickup (administer child's antibiotics before noon cutoff)
  - `13:00 - 15:00`: Hospital Visit & Scan Consent (sign father's authorization at St. Jude)
  - `18:00 - 20:00`: Family Dinner & Child Recovery Care
- **Critical Decision Nodes**:
  - *Decision 1*: Activate Team Delegation: Call Lead Analyst Marcus immediately.
  - *Decision 2*: Hand over final presentation to Marcus and drive to St. Jude to be by your father's side.
  - *Decision 3*: Send a heartfelt recognition note to the team and shut down work for a family meal.
- **Outcome**: Unlocks the master ending: **"THE HARMONIOUS HORIZON"** (3 Stars, 800+ Points).

### 3. Walkthrough: Casefile 02 – The Missing Transaction

- **Goal**: Intercept an unauthorized $12,500 wire before the 15:00 PM bank deadline without destroying partner trust.
- **Recommended Strategy**: 
  - Schedule `Inspect Server Logs` at 08:00 AM, `Interview Sofia` at 09:00 AM, and `Emergency Bank Freeze` at 13:00–15:00 PM.
  - On the decision nodes, opt for calm forensic inquiry rather than aggressive police threats.
  - When spear-phishing is uncovered, submit the cybersecurity incident report directly to the branch manager to execute a federal SWIFT clawback.
- **Outcome**: Unlocks **"THE SHIELD OF CLARITY"** (Full recovery, partnership preserved).

---

### 4. Walkthrough: Casefile 03 – The Family Secret

- **Goal**: Help elderly mother Nirmala navigate an Alzheimer's diagnosis while healing sibling estrangement.
- **Recommended Strategy**:
  - Validate mother's feelings with gentle empathy rather than clinical aggression.
  - Call sister Leena immediately, disclose the unopened letters, and invite her to share caregiving as equal partners.
  - Ensure the revised will divides the estate 50/50, honoring family unity.
- **Outcome**: Unlocks **"THE CIRCLE RESTORED"**.

---

### 5. Walkthrough: Casefile 04 – The Business Crisis

- **Goal**: Launch water monitoring hardware while resisting predatory investor layoffs.
- **Recommended Strategy**:
  - Support Tariq during the 3-hour firmware calibration patch from 08:00–11:00 AM.
  - Stand your ground against Sterling Capital; propose founder salary sacrifices and leverage the state grant.
  - Deliver a transparent, flawless live demonstration to the hospital committee.
- **Outcome**: Unlocks **"THE UNCOMPROMISED MISSION"**.

### 6. Walkthrough: Casefile 05 – The Final Decision

- **Goal**: Synthesize community health needs with municipal development.
- **Recommended Strategy**:
  - Meet protesters peacefully on the City Hall steps without armed escorts.
  - Present the *Integrated Civic Architecture Blueprint* in the closed-door summit to secure a dedicated 3-story modern clinic funded by developer density.
- **Outcome**: Unlocks **"THE COMMONWEALTH COVENANT"** (Master Civic Triumph).
