# GAME CONCEPT DOCUMENT
## LIFE LINES – THE 24-HOUR PUZZLE
> *"Every decision changes the story."*

---

### 1. Executive Summary & Vision
**LIFE LINES – THE 24-HOUR PUZZLE** is a premium, mature interactive strategy and narrative simulation puzzle game created for thoughtful adult players (aged 30 and older). Departing completely from reactionary arcade mechanics, platformers, and coin-collecting tropes, *LIFE LINES* challenges the player to step into the shoes of diverse, highly capable professionals caught in the crosshairs of critical, high-stakes 24-hour emergencies.

In *LIFE LINES*, the puzzle is human life itself: conflicting priorities, limited energy, tight deadlines, fragile relationships, and imperfect information.

---

### 2. Core Pillars

#### Pillar I: Tactical Chronological Scheduling
Time is the most unyielding constraint in human existence. Every scenario takes place over a strict 24-hour cycle. Players must schedule commitments, travel times, negotiations, and medical interventions onto an interactive timeline grid while obeying prerequisites and strict deadlines.

#### Pillar II: Forensic Document Investigation
Complex life problems cannot be solved with snap judgments. Players inspect primary documents—emails, wire slips, medical diagnostics, contracts, and chat logs—to uncover hidden context. Discovering critical evidence unlocks enlightened choices during subsequent decision phases.

#### Pillar III: Multidimensional Resource Equilibrium
Players juggle five interconnected resources:
1. **Time**: Minutes elapsed and remaining in the 24-hour day.
2. **Energy**: Physical endurance and focus (0–100%). Exhaustion impairs performance.
3. **Financial Capital**: Cash, escrow reserves, or funding runway ($).
4. **Stress**: Psychological pressure (0–100%). Unchecked stress causes breakdown.
5. **Relationships**: Trust and rapport with colleagues, partners, family, and clients.

#### Pillar IV: Adaptive Narrative Cascades
No two decisions are inconsequential. The game uses a stateful consequence engine that tracks accumulated flags across multiple choice nodes, resulting in multiple branching endings (Optimal Master, Resilient Compromise, Searing Breakdown).

---

### 3. Target Audience & Persona

- **Primary Demographic**: Adults aged 30–65 (balanced appeal across men and women).
- **Player Motivations**: Intellectual stimulation, emotional resonance, strategic puzzle-solving, realistic adult themes (caregiving, corporate ethics, financial security, community stewardship).
- **Tone & Atmosphere**: Intelligent, mature, cinematic, dignified, and empathetic. Completely devoid of juvenile caricatures or cartoonish tropes.

### 5. Scoring & Evaluation Philosophy
Traditional games reward speed. *LIFE LINES* rewards **foresight, emotional intelligence, and structural integrity**. The 1,000-point rating system breaks down across:
- **Investigative Insight (250 pts)**: Percentage of documents and hidden clues thoroughly inspected.
- **Chronological Precision (300 pts)**: Zero schedule overlaps, zero missed deadlines, and prerequisite compliance.
- **Psychological & Social Equilibrium (250 pts)**: Preserving high energy, low stress, and constructive relationship trust.
- **Narrative Resolution (200 pts)**: Achieving ethical, high-quality resolution endings.
